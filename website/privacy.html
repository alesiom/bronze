Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Introduction

Bronze ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service:

  • Device identifier: A random ID to identify your device for push notifications
  • Favorites: Events you save as favorites
  • Platform: Whether you use iOS or Android

We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal information.

How We Use Your Data

  • Send push notifications about schedule changes for your favorite events
  • Sync your favorites across app reinstalls
  • Improve app reliability and performance

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to deliver notifications:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For Android push notifications (Google)
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs): For iOS push notifications (Apple)

These services receive your device token to deliver notifications. See their respective privacy policies for more information.

Data Retention

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. We periodically review and delete inactive device data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of push notifications (via app settings or device settings)

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at:

hello@bronze.news

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
