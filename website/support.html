Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add an event to my favorites?
    Tap the star icon next to any event to add it to your favorites. You can view all your favorites in the Favorites tab.
  • How do push notifications work?
    When you enable notifications, we'll alert you when the schedule changes for your favorite events - such as time changes, venue changes, or cancellations.
  • Is the app free?
    Yes, the core features (browsing schedules and saving favorites) are free. Push notifications require a one-time purchase.
  • How accurate is the schedule data?
    We update our data regularly from official sources. However, always verify important times with official channels before attending events.
  • How do I change the app language?
    Go to Settings in the app and select your preferred language. We support English, German, and French.
  • Can I get a refund?
    Refunds are handled by the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). Contact their support directly for refund requests.

Response Time

We typically respond to emails within 24-48 hours. During peak event times, responses may take slightly longer.
