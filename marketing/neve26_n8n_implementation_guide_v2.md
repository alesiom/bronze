## Legal Constraints - CRITICAL

### Source
Italian Law Decree 16/2020, converted to Law 31/2020 ("Ambush Marketing Decree")
Official guidelines: https://www.olympics.com/en/milano-cortina-2026/intellectual-property-protection

### Penalties
**€100,000 to €2,500,000** administrative fine for violations.

⚠️ **YOU DO NOT HAVE MONEY FOR THIS. ZERO TOLERANCE FOR RISK.** ⚠️

### Explicitly Protected Terms ❌ NEVER USE

The following are **explicitly prohibited** in any language:

| Category | Protected Terms |
|----------|-----------------|
| Core Olympic terms | Olympic, Olympics, Olympiad, Olimpico, Olimpiade |
| Event names | Milano Cortina 2026, Milano Cortina, Cortina d'Ampezzo + 2026 |
| Year combinations | "twenty twenty-six", "two thousand twenty-six" with Milano/Cortina |
| Official designations | Winter Olympic Games, Olympic Winter Games, Games of 2026, Games of Milano Cortina, XXV Winter Olympic Games |
| Paralympic terms | Paralympic, Paralimpico |
| Mottos | "Altius, Citius, Fortius", "Spirit in Motion" |

### Symbols ❌ NEVER USE
- Olympic rings (5 interlocking circles)
- Paralympic Agitos symbol
- Official Milano Cortina 2026 logos, mascots (Tina & Milo), emblems
- Torch/flame imagery
- Official "Look of the Games" visual identity

### The "Implied Connection" Rule ⚠️ HIGH RISK

Italian law prohibits **any words or references that may indicate a connection** with the Olympic events, even if not explicitly using protected terms.

**These phrases are BANNED:**

| Phrase | Risk Level | Why |
|--------|------------|-----|
| "Winter Games Italy 2026" | ❌ HIGH | Clearly implies the Olympics |
| "Winter Games 2026" | ❌ HIGH | Combined with timing, implies Olympics |
| "The Games" (during Feb 2026) | ❌ HIGH | Context makes it obvious |
| "Italy 2026" | ⚠️ MEDIUM-HIGH | Context-dependent, avoid |
| "February's biggest sporting event" | ❌ HIGH | Implies the Olympics |
| "World's best athletes in Italy" | ⚠️ MEDIUM-HIGH | During event period, implies Olympics |
| "Cortina 2026" | ❌ HIGH | Explicitly protected combination |
| "Milano 2026" | ❌ HIGH | Explicitly protected combination |
| "Going for gold" | ⚠️ MEDIUM | Associated with Olympics |

### What IS Safe ✅

| Category | Safe Examples |
|----------|---------------|
| Generic sport terms | Winter sports, Alpine skiing, Biathlon, Ice hockey |
| City names alone | Milano, Cortina, Bormio, Livigno (WITHOUT 2026) |
| Region names | Dolomiti, Dolomites, Italian Alps |
| Your brand | Neve26, neve26.com |
| Other events | FIS World Cup, Biathlon World Cup, X Games |
| Athlete names | Individual athletes, their career stats |
| Venue names alone | Tofane, Stelvio (without Olympic reference) |

### Athlete Mentions - Special Rules

**ALLOWED:**
- Quoting athletes about their career, mindset, achievements
- Tagging athletes on social media
- Discussing their World Cup/career stats
- Celebrating World Cup wins
- "Mikaela Shiffrin has 100+ World Cup wins"

**NOT ALLOWED:**
- "Watch Shiffrin compete for gold at the Olympics"
- "Team USA's medal hopes"
- Using athlete images in Olympic team gear
- Referencing their Olympic participation directly
- "Shiffrin going for her 3rd Olympic gold"

### Hashtag Rules

**Safe Hashtags ✅**
- #Neve26 (your brand)
- #WinterSports
- #AlpineSkiing, #Biathlon, #FigureSkating, #IceHockey, #Bobsled, #SkiJumping
- #FISAlpine, #FISWorldCup, #IBUBiathlon
- #Cortina, #Milano, #Bormio, #Kitzbühel, #Wengen (cities alone)
- #Dolomiti, #Alps
- Sport event tags: #Adelboden, #Lauberhorn, #Hahnenkamm

**Forbidden Hashtags ❌**
- #Olympics, #Olympic
- #MilanoCortina2026, #Milano2026, #Cortina2026
- #WinterOlympics, #WinterGames
- #TeamUSA, #TeamItaly (NOC trademarks)
- #GoForGold
- #Beijing2022, #Paris2024, #PyeongChang2018 (past Games)

### Safe Content Formulas

**Instead of:** "Track the Winter Olympics schedule"
**Use:** "Track FIS World Cup races and winter sports events"

**Instead of:** "Milano Cortina 2026 event times"
**Use:** "Alpine skiing schedule - Cortina, Bormio, and more"

**Instead of:** "The Games start February 6th"
**Use:** "World Cup racing continues through March"

**Instead of:** "Olympic countdown"
**Use:** "30 days until Neve26 launches" (if referring to app)

### During February 6-22, 2026

The "Olympic Period" has even stricter enforcement:
- Increased monitoring by IOC and Italian authorities
- "Clean zones" around venues
- Rule 40 restrictions on athlete marketing
- **Recommendation: Be EXTRA cautious during this period**
- Focus on World Cup events happening elsewhere
- Avoid any content that could be seen as riding Olympic buzz

### The Bottom Line - Legal

**Focus on:**
- FIS World Cup events (happening now!)
- Biathlon World Cup events
- Athletes' career achievements
- Your app's general utility

**Never imply:**
- Any connection to the Olympics
- Official affiliation with any protected event
- Partnership or sponsorship status
- That you're covering "the big event in Italy"

**When in doubt, don't post it.**

## n8n Automation Architecture

### Workflow 1: Scheduled Quote Posts

**Trigger:** Cron at scheduled times

**Steps:**
1. Read quote from CSV database
2. Select based on time slot category
3. Call Claude API to format for platform
4. Check quote hasn't been used recently
5. Push to Buffer
6. Log used quote

### Workflow 2: Race Day Alerts (Manual Trigger or Scheduled)

**For race days, manually trigger or schedule:**
1. Pull race info (event, time, location)
2. Generate preview post
3. Push to Buffer
4. Schedule result celebration post template

### Workflow 3: Result Celebration (Webhook/Manual)

**When race ends:**
1. Input winner name and handle
2. Generate congratulations post
3. Include athlete tag
4. Push immediately to Buffer

## Instagram Strategy

### Feed vs Stories Distribution

Instagram penalizes excessive feed posts. Distribute across surfaces:

| Surface | Posts/Day | Content Type |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Feed | 2 max | Quote graphics, race results |
| Stories | 5-6 | Race alerts, polls, countdowns, quick facts |
| Reels | 1 optional | Quote with music, highlights |

### Image Generation (for quote posts)

Use ChatGPT/DALL-E with this prompt template:

```
Minimalist quote graphic for Instagram. 
Background: Gradient from glacier blue (#4A90A4) to deep navy (#1E3A5F).
White elegant sans-serif text centered.
Subtle snowflake or mountain silhouette watermark.
Quote: "[QUOTE TEXT]"
Attribution: "— [AUTHOR]"
Aspect ratio: 1:1
No logos, no branding, no text other than the quote.
```
